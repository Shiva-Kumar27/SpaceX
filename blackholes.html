Schwarzschild radius

Formation

Most form when a massive star exhausts its fuel and collapses under its own gravity. If the remaining core exceeds roughly 3 solar masses, no known force can stop the collapse — it continues until the mass occupies effectively zero volume.

Event horizon

The boundary isn't a surface — it's a point of no return. Cross it, and every possible future path leads only inward. Locally, nothing feels unusual at the crossing; the horizon has no texture, no signpost.

The singularity

At the center, general relativity predicts infinite density — a sign the theory is being pushed past where it applies. What actually happens there is one of physics' open questions.
