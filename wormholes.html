Einstein–Rosen bridge

In 1935, Einstein and Rosen found that the equations describing a black hole allow a second solution: a bridge connecting two separate regions of spacetime, rather than a single one-way collapse.

Why it would collapse

A basic wormhole is unstable — it pinches shut faster than anything, even light, could cross it. Keeping it open requires exotic matter with negative energy density, something never directly observed.

Two ends, one object

Some proposals treat entangled black holes as wormholes in disguise — an idea known as ER = EPR, linking the geometry of spacetime to quantum entanglement.
