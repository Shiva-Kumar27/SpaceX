The time-reverse of a black hole: a region nothing can enter, only leave.

The mirror solution

Einstein's equations are time-symmetric, so if a black hole is a valid solution, so is its reverse — matter and light expelled outward, never entering. Run the black hole's story backward and this is what you get.

Why none have been found

A white hole would need to have existed since the beginning of the universe — it can't form from collapse the way a black hole does. Most physicists treat it as a mathematical curiosity rather than a real astrophysical object.

A possible link

Some models pair a white hole with a black hole at the far end of an Einstein-Rosen bridge — matter falling in one side, and, in theory, emerging from the other. This is one root of the wormhole idea.
